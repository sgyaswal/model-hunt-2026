The hunt

Every face on a runway was once a face nobody knew.

MEGA International is casting new faces across India for the 2026 season — for fashion weeks, commercial campaigns, print, and placements with our partner agencies abroad.

You don't need a portfolio, a photographer, or a contact inside the industry. Most of the women we sign walk in with none of those. Bring yourself; we'll handle the rest.

We never charge a fee to audition, to be shortlisted, or to be signed. If anyone asks you for money in our name, write to us.

Questions

Before you ask.

Is there a fee to audition?

No. There is no fee to apply, to audition, to be shortlisted, or to be signed. MEGA International earns a commission on work you're booked for — never from you upfront. Anyone asking you to pay in our name is not us.

I have no photos and no portfolio. Can I still apply?

Yes. Two photographs taken on your phone in daylight are exactly what we want. Studio pictures tell us less, not more.

Is there a height requirement?

Not to apply. Runway work does favour taller frames, but commercial, print, and campaign work does not — and that's most of what we cast for.

I don't live in Mumbai. Can I audition?

Apply from anywhere in India. If you're shortlisted, we'll send your slot with enough notice to travel. Travel and accommodation are your own arrangement.

What should I bring to the audition?

A photo ID, heels if you own them, and simple, fitted clothing. No portfolio required. We'll tell shortlisted applicants everything else in the email.

When will I hear back?

Every application gets a reply, usually within ten days. Shortlists go out on a rolling basis until the Mumbai seats are full.
